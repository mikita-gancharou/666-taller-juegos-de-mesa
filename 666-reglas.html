666

A satanic turn-based sabotage game with cards and dice
Recommended age
12+
Duration
30'
Players
3
Objective of the game:

Be the first player to get all three of your dice to show a value of 6 simultaneously.

Game contents

9Standard d6 dice
29Basic cards
1Demonic die (−2 −1 −1 +2 +2 +3)
11Dual cards
2Rule cards
12Effect cards

Setup

  1. All players roll a d6 die. The player with the highest roll goes first; play continues clockwise. In case of a tie, tied players roll again.
  2. Place the demonic die within reach of all players.
  3. Each player takes three d6 dice and places them in front of themselves showing a value of 3.
  4. Shuffle all cards to form a draw deck placed within reach of everyone.
  5. Place three cards face up side by side to form the "Market".
  6. Deal three cards face down to each player as their starting hand.

Gameplay

On their turn, each player attempts to reach the goal by increasing their own dice values, sabotaging opponents by decreasing theirs, or combining both strategies.

To do so, players have two actions per turn that they may combine as they choose:

• PLAY A CARD: Play a card from your hand.
• If the card modifies your own dice, you choose which of your dice to modify.
• If the card modifies another player's dice, that opponent chooses which of their dice is modified.
Limit rule: Values cannot go below 1 or above 6. If a reduction must be applied when all target dice show 1, no die is modified.
• DRAW A CARD: Take a card from the market. The maximum hand size limit is 5 cards. If a player reaches this limit, they cannot perform this action.
Dice lock upon reaching 6:

When a player changes at least one of their dice to a 6, that die becomes locked and can only be modified by effect cards. In addition, the player draws a free card and gains an additional action on their subsequent turns until an opponent reduces the value of all the 6s they own.

End of Turn and Market: Players must perform all available actions to end their turn. The market is replenished from the draw deck ONCE THE TURN IS ENDED if any card was drawn, ensuring the next turn always starts with three cards in the market. If the draw deck runs out, shuffle the discard pile to form a new draw deck.

Card types

BASIC
Adds value to one of your own dice, or subtracts value from an opponent's die.
DUAL
Adds value to one of your own dice while simultaneously subtracting value from an opponent's die.

Effect cards

The target die for effect cards is decided by the player using the card.
SMASH
Reduces an opponent's locked die with a value of 6 directly to 5.
REROLL
Roll one of your own dice and keep whatever value lands face up.
PACT
Choose one of your own dice and roll the demonic die. On a negative result, set the chosen die to 1. On a positive result, set it to 6.
GAMBLE
Choose a die — yours or an opponent's — then roll the demonic die and apply the rolled value to that die.
SHIELD
REACTION
Cancels an attack played by another player targeting your locked die. Can be played at any time without counting as an action.
END OF THE GAME

The game ends immediately when one player achieves the goal, granting them eternal rest while the remaining players suffer eternal punishment in hell.
